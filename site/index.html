Your music library, one hotkey away.

HotSonos lives in the Windows system tray and gives you global hotkeys for the music files you share to Sonos: history-aware shuffle, play/pause, skip, and whole-house volume, over your local network. No cloud and no Sonos account. Shuffle does not work with Spotify, Apple Music, or other streaming services.

Download for WindowsView on GitHub

Free for Windows 10 and 11. Per-user MSI installer, .NET runtime included.

For your own music library

Local files you already share to Sonos. HotSonos groups your speakers and shuffles that library, leaving out songs you've heard recently. Shuffle, top-up, exclusions, and Restart fresh do not work with Spotify, Apple Music, or other streaming services.

🔀

History-aware shuffle

Groups your speakers and builds a short random mix from your own music library, leaving out songs you've heard recently. When it runs low, a fresh batch is added automatically. Streaming services are not part of that mix.

⌨️

Global hotkeys

Shuffle, play/pause, next, previous, volume up/down, and mute from any app. Every shortcut is re-bindable.

🔉

Whole-house volume

One "house" volume level for every room, with per-room offsets for amp-fed speakers, plus a one-click Level all.

🔗

Grouping & Daily groups

Shuffle to all speakers or a checked subset of rooms, save named Daily speaker groups, and pick which room leads the group.

🎵

Now-playing flyout

Album art, title, artist, and state in a draggable, pinnable flyout that updates live from your speakers.

🏷️

Tags, genres & folders

Tag tracks into moods like Dinner or Focus, then play a tag, genre, or library folder as its own shuffle. Quick Tag and Quick Play overlays keep it keyboard-first.

📊

Reports

Song play history, most played and most skipped songs, completed vs. skipped outcomes, and an event log of music and speaker activity.

☀️

Wake to music

Schedule music to start in a room with a gentle volume ramp, from a favorite or a shuffle, optionally expanding to the whole house.

🤖

Local MCP server

An optional loopback MCP endpoint on this PC lets AI assistants search your library and control playback.

Also: favorite slots with their own hotkeys, Restart fresh (re-sync speakers and reshuffle), and live speaker monitoring with auto-rejoin.

Requirements

That's it — no account, no subscription.

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11
  • Sonos speakers (S1 or S2) on the same local network as the PC
  • For library shuffle and tagging: network (SMB) access from the PC to the music share your Sonos system indexes
  • For wake-up music and nightly re-sync: the PC awake with HotSonos running

FAQ

Does HotSonos need a Sonos account or the internet?

No. It finds and controls your speakers directly on your local network, the same way the speakers talk to each other. There's no HotSonos account and no HotSonos server.

Does it work with Spotify or other streaming services?

HotSonos is built for your own music library on Sonos: local files shared to your speakers. History-aware shuffle, top-up, exclusions, and Restart fresh reshuffle use that library. They do not work with Spotify, Apple Music, or other streaming services. Play, pause, skip, and volume are sent straight to the speakers, so they still control the group while something else is already playing.

Does it replace the Sonos app?

No — it sits alongside it. Use the Sonos app for setup and music services. Use HotSonos for keyboard-first control of your own music library.

How does the shuffle avoid repeats?

As songs play or you skip them, HotSonos remembers them and leaves them out of the next batch (by default, roughly the last couple of weeks). If you've heard most of your library, it loosens that rule so music keeps playing.

Is there a Mac or phone version?

The tray app is Windows. The Android app works either as a remote for that tray app or standalone, talking to your speakers on the same Wi-Fi. It also runs on a Chromebook.

iPhone and iPad: early prototype. There is no Mac app.

My speakers stutter or drop out of groups. Can HotSonos help?

Restart fresh re-discovers and regroups speakers and starts a new shuffle. If problems persist, the README explains why turning off SonosNet and choosing a 5 GHz group coordinator often helps.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The MSI isn't code-signed yet. SmartScreen may show a prompt; choose More info → Run anyway.

Is it free?

Yes. HotSonos is open source under the MIT license. The source and every release are on GitHub.
